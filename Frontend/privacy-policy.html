🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025 · Effective immediately

Your privacy matters deeply to us. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it. MindCare is a mental wellness platform — we treat your health information with the highest level of care.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account data: Name, email address, password (hashed, never stored in plain text)
  • Profile data: Age, gender, location (optional), mental health concerns you share
  • Session data: Appointment records, session types, therapist communications
  • Payment data: Processed securely via Stripe — we never store card numbers
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, device type (for improving the platform)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match you with suitable therapists based on your preferences
  • To process session bookings and payments
  • To send session reminders and important account notifications
  • To improve our platform and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Mental Health Data — Special Protection

Information related to your mental health, therapy sessions, and communications with therapists is treated as sensitive health data. We apply the following protections:

  • Session notes are private and only accessible to the therapist who wrote them
  • We do not share your health information with third parties for advertising purposes
  • Conversation content is end-to-end encrypted in transit
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your therapist: Your name, session details, and messages you send them
  • Payment processor (Stripe): Only what's needed to complete your transaction
  • Email service: Your email address, for sending reminders and notifications
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to prevent imminent harm

We never share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we cannot see your password
  • Payment information is processed by Stripe and never touches our servers
  • Access to production data is restricted to essential personnel only

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update any incorrect information in your profile
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from non-essential communications at any time

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Session records are retained for 3 years for legal and medical compliance. You may request earlier deletion, subject to legal obligations.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

MindCare is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us immediately.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related requests or concerns, contact us at:

Email: privacy@mindcare.com
Response time: Within 5 business days

This policy was last reviewed in January 2025. We will notify registered users of any material changes.
