🧑‍⚕️ Therapist Code of Conduct

All therapists on MindCare are expected to uphold these professional standards.

1. Professional Qualifications

  • ✓Hold a valid license or registration from a recognized mental health council (RCI, MCI, or equivalent)
  • ✓Keep your license current and renew it as required by your regulatory body
  • ✓Only practice within your areas of training and competence
  • ✓Disclose any changes to your licensure status to MindCare immediately

2. Client Welfare

The wellbeing of your client must always be your primary concern. Never let commercial interests override clinical judgment.
  • ✓Conduct a proper intake assessment before beginning ongoing therapy
  • ✓Refer clients to appropriate emergency services if there is risk to life
  • ✓Maintain continuity of care — do not abandon a client without proper transition
  • ✓Be transparent about your therapeutic approach and its limitations

3. Confidentiality

  • ✓Maintain strict confidentiality of all client information
  • ✓Only break confidentiality when required by law or to prevent imminent serious harm
  • ✓Inform clients upfront about the limits of confidentiality
  • ✗Never share client information with family members without written consent

4. Professional Boundaries

Boundary violations are grounds for immediate removal from MindCare and may result in reporting to your licensing body.
  • ✗Never engage in romantic or sexual relationships with clients — current or former
  • ✗Never accept gifts of significant value from clients
  • ✗Never conduct sessions while impaired by substances
  • ✗Never exploit the therapeutic relationship for personal or financial gain outside the platform
  • ✗Never attempt to solicit clients to move off the MindCare platform to avoid fees

5. Session Standards

  • ✓Begin and end sessions on time as scheduled
  • ✓Conduct sessions in a private, professional environment
  • ✓Ensure reliable internet connectivity for video sessions
  • ✓Notify clients and MindCare as early as possible if you need to cancel
  • ✓Maintain a professional appearance and demeanor

6. Record Keeping

  • ✓Maintain appropriate clinical notes for each session
  • ✓Store any personal records securely and confidentially
  • ✓Retain session records for a minimum of 3 years

7. Continuing Education

  • ✓Engage in regular supervision or peer consultation
  • ✓Pursue continuing education to stay current with best practices
  • ✓Seek personal therapy or support when experiencing burnout or vicarious trauma

8. Platform Conduct

  • ✓Respond to client messages within 24 hours during working days
  • ✓Maintain accurate and up-to-date profile information
  • ✓Report any client behaviour that poses a safety risk
  • ✗Never post false or misleading information in your profile
  • ✗Never write or solicit fake reviews

9. Violations and Consequences

Violations of this code may result in:

  • Warning and required corrective action
  • Temporary suspension from the platform
  • Permanent removal from MindCare
  • Reporting to the relevant licensing body where legally required
